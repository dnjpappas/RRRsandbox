Welcome to

ROUGH ROAD RANCH

Rough Road Ranch began with my special needs daughter. I wanted to create a place where her days could have purpose, activity, and something she could be proud to be part of. What started with a few goats and chickens became our family farm—a place built around caring for animals and finding joy in the simple rhythms of farm life.

Today, we're opening our farm to a small number of horse owners who are looking for something a little different from a traditional boarding facility. With spacious grounds, established stalls, shaded pastures, and a peaceful barn beneath Florida's live oaks, Rough Road Ranch offers a quieter, more personal place for horses to call home.

HORSE BOARDING

A Home for the Quieter Horse

Flexible options and personalized daily care allow you to choose the boarding arrangement and level of support that best fits you and your horse. Whether you prefer stall or pasture boarding, and whether you want full care or prefer to handle daily care yourself, Rough Road Ranch offers a peaceful, personal setting with the support you need.

Rough Road Ranch is especially suited for retired and geriatric horses, companion horses, miniature horses and ponies, and medically cleared horses with manageable special needs. We welcome horses who may benefit from a slower pace and peaceful environment, including those with vision or hearing loss, joint limitations, or other age-related considerations.

Your Feed, Our Care

You choose and supply your horse's preferred feed, keeping you in control of their diet and feed costs. When feeding is included in your care plan, we feed according to your instructions. Coastal hay and fresh water are provided by Rough Road Ranch; owners who prefer premium hay, such as alfalfa, supply their own.

Your Horse, Your Instructions

Each horse has an individual care board with your specific feeding and care instructions, helping ensure your horse's routine is followed consistently.

THE BARN

Located in Umatilla, Florida, Rough Road Ranch is a five-acre family farm where we board horses and raise goats and chickens. At the heart of the property is our four-stall, open-air barn, designed for Florida living with generous ventilation, covered stalls, and a wide central aisle.

The property offers a variety of spaces for horses to enjoy throughout the day. Our one-acre front paddock is shaded by mature trees that provide natural shelter from the Florida sun and weather, while three open acres of grassy back pasture offer plenty of room for grazing. An additional acre surrounding the barn connects the spaces and keeps daily care close at hand.
